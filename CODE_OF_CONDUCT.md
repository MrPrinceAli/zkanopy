# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported privately to the maintainer, [@MrPrinceAli](https://github.com/MrPrinceAli). All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour deemed inappropriate.
